Connect a WhatsApp number to Hermes

A short guide covering what to prepare on Meta's side before you start, and the steps to complete inside Hermes.

Before you start — outside Hermes

WhatsApp Business numbers are owned by you in Meta, not by us. To connect a number to Hermes, you need a small amount of setup on Meta's side first. None of these steps happen inside Hermes.

What you need

  • A Meta Business Manager account in your company's name. If you don't have one yet, create it at business.facebook.com. Fill in your business address, website, and contact email — Meta uses these during verification.
  • A phone number you control, capable of receiving SMS or a voice call. Meta will send a one-time code to this number during signup.
  • The number must not be in active use on the consumer WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business mobile app. If it is, delete the WhatsApp account on that device before continuing — Meta will refuse to register the number otherwise.
  • Admin rights on your Business Manager. The person completing the signup needs to be a business admin in Meta — only admins can authorize a new WhatsApp Business Account.

Special case — your number already has a WhatsApp Business Account

If the number you want to use is already registered as a WABA somewhere (with another provider like Twilio, 360dialog, Vonage, or directly with Meta), that's fine — Meta's signup flow will handle the migration. You'll need:

  • The two-step verification PIN currently set on the existing WABA, or the ability to reset it via Meta's recovery options.
  • To know which provider currently owns the number (helpful for support, not strictly required).
Heads up Meta limits how many migration attempts a number can have in a short window. If a migration fails, wait at least a few hours before retrying — repeated failures can lock the number out temporarily.

What you do not need

  • You do not need an existing WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID) — Hermes captures it for you during signup.
  • You do not need an Infobip account. Hermes handles the messaging backend for you.
  • You do not need to pre-create message templates — you can add those after the number is connected.

What to do inside Hermes

Once the prerequisites above are in place, the rest of the work happens inside Hermes. The whole flow takes about five minutes if your Business Manager is already verified.

  1. Open the WhatsApp onboarding screen. Go to Channels → WhatsApp → Add number in the Hermes admin UI.
  2. Click Launch Embedded Signup. A popup window opens — this is Meta's signup flow, hosted by Meta directly. Hermes does not collect your Meta credentials.
  3. Log in to Facebook. Use the Facebook account associated with your Business Manager. If you're already logged in, Meta will skip this step.
  4. Select or create your Business Manager and WhatsApp Business Account. If you have multiple Business Managers, pick the right one. If this is your first WABA, Meta will help you create one inside the popup.
  5. Add and verify your phone number. Enter the number, choose SMS or voice verification, then enter the code Meta sends you. If your number already has an existing WABA, Meta will prompt you for the two-step PIN to migrate it.
  6. Confirm the permissions. Meta asks you to grant Hermes permission to manage WhatsApp Business assets and send messages on your behalf. Accept, then close the popup.
  7. Wait for registration to finish. Hermes will show the new number in your channel list with an in progress status. Meta usually completes registration in under a minute, but it can take up to a few minutes during busy periods.
  8. You're live. When the status flips to finished, the number is ready to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
What success looks like The number appears in Channels → WhatsApp with status finished, a display name, and a phone-number ID. You can immediately start sending free-form messages to opted-in users and submit message templates for Meta approval.

Registration statuses you might see

StatusMeaningWhat to do
in progress Meta has accepted the registration and is finalising it. Normal for the first 1–2 minutes after signup. Wait. No action needed.
finished The number is fully registered. You can send messages. Move on to templates and contacts.
failed Something went wrong on Meta's side — most commonly the number was in use on the consumer WhatsApp app, the migration PIN was wrong, or Meta hit a rate limit. Check the failure reason shown in Hermes. Resolve the issue (e.g. delete consumer WhatsApp, get the correct PIN, wait out the rate limit) and re-run the signup.

After your number is connected

  • Submit message templates. Most outbound business-initiated traffic on WhatsApp requires a pre-approved template. Add yours in Channels → WhatsApp → Templates and Meta will review (typically within 24 hours).
  • Set the business profile. Display name, business description, profile picture, and category all live in the channel settings. These are visible to your end users in WhatsApp.
  • Verify your business with Meta (recommended). Verification unlocks higher messaging limits and is required for the green checkmark. You can start this from your Business Manager any time.

If you get stuck

The most common blockers and how to resolve them:

  • "This number cannot be used" — almost always means the number is active on the consumer WhatsApp app on a phone somewhere. Delete the account in the WhatsApp app, then retry.
  • "Two-step verification PIN incorrect" — Meta lets you reset the PIN from WhatsApp Manager → Settings → Two-step verification. If you don't have access there, your current provider can help, or you can wait 7 days for Meta's automatic recovery to kick in.
  • "You need business verification first" — Meta requires your Business Manager to be verified before allowing a WABA in some cases. Start verification from Business Settings → Security Center in Meta Business Manager. It usually takes 2–5 business days.
  • Registration stuck on in progress for more than 10 minutes — contact your Hermes administrator. They can check the registration event log on Infobip's side.
